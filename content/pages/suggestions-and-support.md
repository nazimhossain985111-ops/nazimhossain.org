## Support accessible gaming {#support}

I develop audio games that blind and visually impaired players can enjoy alongside everyone else. Keeping these games running has real costs: the game server has to stay online every day, tournaments need prizes, and the people who help run the games deserve to be paid for their time. If you would like to support this work, any contribution helps, and all of it goes to these costs. Thank you for supporting games that everyone can play.
