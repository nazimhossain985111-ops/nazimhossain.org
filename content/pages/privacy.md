This is a personal, non-commercial website. It does not use cookies, does not show advertising and does not track you across other websites. This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit the website, write to me or send a form.

## Visiting the website

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like every web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the pages and protect the website from attacks. I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies or collect personal information, and it shows me only totals, such as how many people visited a page.

The fonts and the search are served by this website itself, so nothing is sent to font services or search providers. Your searches stay in your browser.

## Forms and email

When you send a form, the details you enter are emailed to me through Cloudflare. They are not stored on the website. Messages sent to contact@nazimhossain.org are forwarded by Cloudflare to my personal mailbox, which is provided by Google.

I use your name, email address, phone number and message only to reply to you and to help with your request. I do not sell or share them, and I do not add you to any mailing list. You may ask me at any time to delete your messages.

You can send the suggestion form without a name or email address if you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Translation and downloads

If you choose a language other than English, the page is translated by Google Translate, and Google receives the address of the page. Google's privacy policy applies to translated pages.

The Race Master download is provided through GitHub, which receives the usual technical information when you download a file. GitHub's privacy statement applies to downloads.

## Questions

For any question about your information, email [contact@nazimhossain.org](mailto:contact@nazimhossain.org).

This policy was last updated on 10 October 2026.
